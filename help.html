ColorPress – How to use

Turn your own photos into coloring pages and print a whole coloring book – with a drawing AI that runs on your PC.

1. Add photos

Click 📷 Add photos, drop pictures onto the window, or paste one with Ctrl+V. Every photo becomes one page. The first time, the drawing AI is downloaded once (about 17 MB); after that ColorPress works offline. Want to see it first? Click ✨ Try an example.

2. Make the drawing just right

  • Simple · Classic · Detailed – quick choices: Simple gives big shapes and thick lines for little children, Detailed keeps more lines for older children and grown-ups.
  • Detail – more or fewer lines.
  • Line thickness – thick lines are easier to colour inside.
  • Remove small bits – takes away dots and short scratches (grass, hair, fur).
  • Smooth the photo first – for busy photos (fur, leaves, patterns): the photo is smoothed before it is drawn, so there are fewer scratchy lines.
  • Tick Use these settings for every page to change all pages at once.
  • Tick Show the photo next to it to compare with the original photo.

3. The page

  • Paper: US Letter or A4 for home printing. Pro adds book sizes for Amazon KDP (8.5 × 11 in with or without bleed, 8 × 10, 6 × 9).
  • Auto / Portrait / Landscape: Auto turns the page to fit each photo.
  • Words on the page: a name or a title in big hollow letters – children can colour the letters too.
  • Frame: a rounded frame around the picture.

4. A coloring book

All pages are listed under 📚 Coloring book. Click a page to change it, use ‹ › to change the order and ✕ to remove it. Your pages are kept on this PC, so they are still there the next time you open ColorPress. With Pro you can add a cover page with the book title, page numbers and a blank back of every page (so markers do not bleed through when you print single-sided pages for a book).

5. Print or save

  • 🖨 Print (Ctrl+P) prints the whole book. In the print window choose your printer; the paper size is set already.
  • 📄 Save PDF (Ctrl+S) saves the whole book as one PDF in your Downloads folder – to print in a copy shop or upload to Amazon KDP.
  • ☰ More → Save this page as a picture saves one page as a PNG.
For Amazon KDP: choose the KDP paper size (with bleed if your book has pictures to the edge – most coloring books do not need bleed), tick Blank back of every page and upload the PDF as the interior of your book.

6. Free version and Pro

The free version turns any number of photos into coloring pages and prints or saves books of up to 3 pages at 150 dpi on US Letter or A4 – with no watermark. Pro is a one-time purchase – no subscription – and adds unlimited pages, sharp 300 dpi pages, KDP book sizes, a cover page, page numbers and blank backs.

7. Tips for the best pages

  • Photos with one clear subject work best: a face, a pet, a house, a car, a flower.
  • Crop the photo close to the subject before you add it.
  • Busy background? Raise Remove small bits and Smooth the photo first.
  • Faces look best large: a close portrait gives a nicer page than a person far away.

8. If something goes wrong

  • “The drawing AI is downloaded the first time…”: connect to the internet once and open the page again. Until then ColorPress makes a simpler drawing without AI.
  • Drawing is slow: the first drawing of a page takes a few seconds; changing Detail, Thickness or Small bits afterwards is quick.
  • Nothing happens when I print: use the 🖨 Print button in ColorPress (or Ctrl+P), not the browser menu.
