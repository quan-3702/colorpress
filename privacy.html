ColorPress privacy policy

ColorPress works entirely on your own computer. Your photos and coloring pages are never uploaded, collected or shared. They are kept only on your PC (in the app's own storage) until you remove them. The app has no account, no advertising and no tracking.

The first time you use it, the drawing AI (about 17 MB) and its runtime are downloaded once and stored on your PC so the app works offline afterwards. No photo or personal information is sent with that download.

Purchases of ColorPress Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store.
